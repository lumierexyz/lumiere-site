Vision

What is Lumiere?

Lumiere is building toward a personal AI financial agent that understands an individual’s spending patterns, estimates future living costs, and helps construct personalized strategies for managing purchasing power risk.

The long-term vision is financial stability measured against real-world needs rather than a single currency.

Why measure purchasing power?

Stablecoins hold their value against a currency such as the US dollar. Purchasing power describes what that balance can actually buy. When housing, food, energy, or transportation become more expensive, a stable balance can still lose real value.

What is available today?

Lumiere is in research and early development. Personalized expense modeling and the AI agent are planned functionality. Market-based instruments for purchasing power are a research direction, not a launched product.

What does Lumiere not do?

Lumiere does not guarantee purchasing power, offer returns, manage user assets, or execute trades on anyone’s behalf.

It has not launched a stablecoin or a prediction market, and nothing on this site is financial advice.
